Our Story

Every Woman Has a Season to Rise

Cicada Rise began with three children who grew up watching their mothers—women who worked tirelessly, put their families first, and quietly carried dreams of their own. We saw their strength in the little things: their resilience, their sacrifices, their creativity, and the love they poured into everything they did. Yet, like so many women, much of their story remained unseen.

Those moments stayed with us and inspired us to build something meaningful.

Like the cicada, which spends years growing quietly beneath the surface before rising, transforming, and taking flight, we believe every woman has her own season to rise. Growth is often silent. Confidence is built through patience. Transformation comes from embracing who you are.

At Cicada Rise, we celebrate the quiet resilience of women who are stepping beyond the unseen, turning their skills into confidence and their dreams into independence. Our collections are more than clothing—they are a reminder that every woman deserves to feel seen, empowered, and beautiful in every chapter of her journey.

Every stitch tells a story.

Every outfit carries purpose.

Every woman has a season to rise.
